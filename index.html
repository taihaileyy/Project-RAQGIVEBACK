Our Mission

You don't have to let your environment define you.

RaqGiveback takes RAQ's message — that you can use the positive things around you to better your circumstances — and puts it into action. Through mentorship, community assistance, and partnership with local organizations, we help kids and families build real opportunity.

Mentorship

We match kids and teens with mentors who help with life skills, faith, career, and education.

Direct Support

Food, clothing, shelter referrals, job assistance, and more for people in our community.

Partnership

We work alongside churches, businesses, nonprofits, and shelters to multiply our impact.
