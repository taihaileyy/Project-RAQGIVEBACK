Our Mission

You don't have to let your environment define you.

RaqGiveback takes RAQ's message — that you can use the positive things around you to better your circumstances — and puts it into action.

Through mentorship, community assistance, and partnership with local organizations, we help kids and families build real opportunity. Every application is reviewed by our team, and every mentor is screened before they're matched with a young person.
